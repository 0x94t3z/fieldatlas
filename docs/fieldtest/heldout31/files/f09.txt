Grandma Edie's sourdough (my version, updated after 6 bakes)

Makes 1 large boule. Baker's %: flour 100, water 75, levain 20, salt 2.

Ingredients
- 400 g strong white bread flour
- 100 g wholemeal flour
- 375 g water (about 27 C) + 10 g held back
- 100 g active levain (fed 1:1:1 the night before, should be bubbly and domed, passes float test)
- 10 g fine sea salt

Night before (9 pm)
Feed the starter: 20 g starter + 50 g flour + 50 g water. Leave on the counter. By 8-9 am it should have doubled.

Day 1
08:30 Autolyse - mix the flours with 365 g of the water until no dry bits. Cover and leave 45-60 min. Don't add salt or levain yet.
09:30 Add the levain, dimple it in, squish together. Rest 20 min.
09:50 Add salt + the held-back 10 g water, pinch and fold until it's absorbed. This is where it gets sticky - wet hands!
10:15 - 12:15 Bulk fermentation starts. 4 sets of stretch and folds every 30 min (grab one side, pull up, fold over, turn the bowl, repeat x4). Then leave it alone.
Bulk total ~4.5-5 h at 24 C. Look for ~50% rise, domed edges, bubbles on the side of the container, jiggly. Kitchen in winter is 19 C so it can take 7 h - go by the dough, not the clock!!
14:30 Pre-shape into a loose round, bench rest 25 min uncovered.
15:00 Final shape (tight boule, build surface tension), into a rice-floured banneton seam side up.
Cold retard in the fridge overnight, 12-16 h.

Day 2
- Oven + Dutch oven preheat at 250 C for 45 min.
- Turn dough out onto parchment, score with a lame (one long curved slash at ~30 degrees for an ear).
- Bake lid ON 20 min at 250 C, lid OFF 20-25 min at 230 C until deep brown. Internal temp ~96-98 C.
- Cool at least 1 hour before cutting!!! (gummy crumb otherwise)

Troubleshooting log
- Bake 2: flat, spread out. Probably over-proofed in bulk (left 7h in warm kitchen).
- Bake 4: tight crumb, big tunnels near the top = under-fermented.
- Bake 6: best one yet. Ear, open crumb.
