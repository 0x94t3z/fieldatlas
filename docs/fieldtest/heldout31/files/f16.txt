QUOTATION - Air source heat pump installation
Northgate Renewables Ltd, MCS cert no. NG-114822
Customer: Mr & Mrs D. Owusu, 14 Fernhill Close, Chesterfield
Quote ref: NR-2026-0913   Valid 60 days   Date: 29 September 2026

Property survey summary
Semi-detached, built 1976, 118 m2, cavity walls (filled 2011), loft insulation topped to 300 mm in 2019, uPVC double glazing. Current heating: 2008 non-condensing gas boiler, 15 radiators.
Heat loss calculation (MCS / BS EN 12831): 7.4 kW at a design external temperature of -2.3 C and internal 21 C. Hot water demand for 4 occupants: 180 L/day.

Proposed system
- 8 kW monobloc air source heat pump, R290 refrigerant, outdoor unit to the side elevation on anti-vibration feet.
- 210 L unvented cylinder with a 3.0 m2 coil, in the airing cupboard.
- Weather compensation controls, flow temperature 45 C at design condition.
- Radiator upgrades: 6 radiators to be replaced with larger K2/K3 panels to work at the lower flow temperature. Living room to have a fan-assisted convector.
- New 22 mm primary pipework from the outdoor unit, magnetic filter, and system flush (BS 7593).
- Volumiser 40 L to protect against short cycling during defrost.

Performance
- Quoted SCOP 3.6 (at 45 C flow, per manufacturer EN 14825 data).
- Estimated annual electricity use for heating and hot water: 4,400 kWh.
- Estimated noise at nearest neighbour boundary: 38 dB(A), within permitted development limits.
