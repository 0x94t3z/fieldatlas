LISBON - family trip plan v3 (Rui, Ana, kids Leo 9 + Maya 6)

Flights
Out: Sat 7 Nov, Manchester -> Lisbon, dep 06:55, arr 09:50 local. Booking ref KX7QP2.
Back: Thu 12 Nov, dep 18:40 Lisbon -> Manchester.
Remember: Portugal is on the same time as the UK.

Hotel
Casa do Largo, Rua de Sao Miguel, Alfama (4 nights, family room, breakfast included). Check-in from 14:00 - they will hold bags if we arrive early. Note: lots of steps, no lift, so pack light.
Night 5 (Wed 11 Nov): moving to Hotel Estrela Sol near Cais do Sodre so we are closer to the Time Out Market and the airport metro.

Getting around
- Viva Viagem cards (0.50 EUR each) loaded with "zapping" credit. Leo and Maya need their own cards.
- Metro red line from the airport to Alameda, then green line.

Day 1 Sat: arrive, drop bags, lunch in Alfama, Sao Jorge castle in the afternoon (tickets booked 15:30), sunset at Miradouro de Santa Luzia.

Day 2 Sun: Tram 28 - aim for the 08:40 from Martim Moniz before the queues (Ana says by 10 it is an hour wait). Get off at Estrela, playground in Jardim da Estrela. Afternoon: Oceanario, tickets for 14:00, ref OCN-55813.

Day 3 Mon: Belem. Train from Cais do Sodre. Pasteis de Belem before 10, Jeronimos Monastery, Torre de Belem, Padrao dos Descobrimentos, MAAT if the kids are still alive.

Day 4 Tue: Sintra day trip. Train from Rossio at 08:11, about 40 min. Pena Palace timed entry 10:30 (booked). Then Quinta da Regaleira and the initiation well. Back by 6 pm. Rui's birthday dinner at Taberna da Rua das Flores, booked 19:30 for 4.

Day 5 Wed: lazy morning, beach at Cascais by train if the weather holds, otherwise LX Factory and Pink Street. Change hotels.

Day 6 Thu: Time Out Market for lunch, airport by 16:00.

Budget: 1,600 EUR spending money. Already paid: flights 742 GBP, hotels 890 GBP.

To pack: sun hats, Maya's travel sickness bands for the Sintra bus, type F adaptor, swimming stuff for Cascais.
