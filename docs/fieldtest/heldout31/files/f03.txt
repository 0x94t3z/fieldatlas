If you miss a dose
- If you remember on the same day, take it as soon as you remember.
- If you only remember the next day, skip the missed dose and take your normal dose for that day. Never take a double dose to make up.
- Write the missed dose in your yellow book and tell the nurse at your next test.

Contact the clinic the same day if you have
- Bleeding that does not stop after 10 minutes of pressure, blood in urine or black stools, coughing or vomiting blood.
- A fall or a bang to the head (go to A&E if you hit your head).
- Unusual bruising.

Things that can change your INR
- Starting or stopping any medicine, including antibiotics, and over-the-counter painkillers such as ibuprofen or aspirin. Paracetamol is usually fine at normal doses but tell us if you take it every day.
- Herbal remedies such as St John's wort, ginkgo, and cranberry juice in large amounts.
- Big changes in leafy green vegetables (vitamin K). Keep your diet steady rather than avoiding greens.
- Alcohol: no more than 1-2 units a day, and avoid binge drinking.
