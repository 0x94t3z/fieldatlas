What a normal day looks like
- Wakes 06:30ish, always confused for the first hour, don't argue, just open the curtains and make tea (two sugars, in the blue mug - it has to be the blue mug).

- Sleeps better if no TV after 20:00 and the hall light is left on.

Important

- He still drives the mobility scooter on the pavement - that is allowed and he is safe with it, but not after dark.
- If he gets suddenly much worse over a day or two (more confused than normal, drowsy, hot, smelly urine), ring the GP the same day. Last time that happened it was a urine infection and he was back to baseline in a week.
