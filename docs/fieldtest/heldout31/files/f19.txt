Saturday walk plan - Helvellyn via Striding Edge (group of 5, two first-timers on scrambles)

Date: Sat 25 Oct. Meet Glenridding main car park 08:00 (pay and display, card machine broken last time, bring coins). Alternative parking: Glenridding village hall if full.

Route
Glenridding -> Mires Beck path -> Birkhouse Moor -> Hole-in-the-Wall -> Striding Edge -> Helvellyn summit (950 m) -> Swirral Edge down -> Catstycam optional -> Red Tarn -> back via Mires Beck.
Distance ~13 km, ascent ~900 m. Guide time 6-7 h, call it 8 with the group we have.
Grid ref for Hole-in-the-Wall: NY 35845 15200. Summit shelter NY 342 151.

Timings
08:30 leave car park
10:15 Hole-in-the-Wall (first proper break, layer up, it is always windy there)
10:30-12:00 Striding Edge
12:15 summit, lunch in the cross shelter
13:00 start Swirral Edge
14:30 Red Tarn
16:00 back at the cars. Pub: Travellers Rest, book for 16:30 just in case.

Safety
- Striding Edge is Grade 1 scramble, exposed on both sides, and the "bad step" near the end catches people out. If it is wet or gusting over 35 mph we switch to the Swirral Edge up-and-down or just do the pony route from Thirlspot.
- Forecast check: MWIS and Mountain Forecast the night before AND at 07:00. Freezing level matters - if it is below 800 m there could be verglas on the rock and we bail. None of us has axes or crampons this early in the season.
- Sunset 17:55 BST but the clocks go back that night, check this. Everyone brings a head torch regardless.
- No reliable phone signal on the ridge or in Red Tarn Cove. Signal at Hole-in-the-Wall sometimes.
- Water: fill up before, there is nothing after Mires Beck. 2 L each.
- Emergency: 999 ask for police then mountain rescue, or 112, or SMS 999 if registered. Patterdale MRT covers this.

Kit
Boots with ankle support, waterproofs top and bottom, hat and gloves, spare warm layer, map (OL5) and compass, whistle, first aid kit, 1 group shelter, flask.

Jen is nervous about the exposure. Tell her: there is a lower path on the north side that avoids the crest for most of the ridge, and we can turn around at any point up to the bad step.
