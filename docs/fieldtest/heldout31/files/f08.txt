LODGER AGREEMENT (excerpt - clauses 1 to 9)

Property: 27 Chandos Road, Redland, Bristol BS6
Householder: Fiona Marsh ("the Householder"), who lives at the Property as her only home
Lodger: Tomasz Wiśniewski ("the Lodger")
Room: second-floor front bedroom, with shared use of kitchen, bathroom and garden

1. Term
1.1 The agreement starts on 1 September 2026 for a fixed term of twelve months, ending on 31 August 2027.
1.2 After the fixed term the agreement continues month to month until ended by either party under clause 8.

2. Rent and bills
2.1 Rent is £725 per calendar month, payable in advance on the 1st of each month by bank transfer.
2.2 Rent includes gas, electricity, water, council tax and broadband.
2.3 If rent is more than 14 days late, the Householder may charge interest at 3% above the Bank of England base rate.

3. Deposit
3.1 The Lodger has paid a deposit of £725.
3.2 The deposit will be returned within 21 days of the end of the agreement, less any reasonable deductions for unpaid rent or damage beyond fair wear and tear.

4. Lodger's obligations
4.1 Keep the room clean and tidy and help keep shared areas clean (rota on the fridge).
4.2 No smoking or vaping anywhere inside the Property.
4.3 Overnight guests no more than two nights in any week, and the Householder must be told in advance.
4.4 No pets without the Householder's written consent.

5. Householder's obligations
5.1 Keep the room and shared areas in good repair and provide a working smoke alarm and carbon monoxide alarm.
5.2 Give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the room except in an emergency.

6. Keys
6.1 Two keys provided. Lost keys replaced at the Lodger's cost (£40 per key).

7. Quiet hours
7.1 Quiet hours are 11 pm to 7 am Sunday to Thursday.

8. Ending the agreement
8.1 Break clause: during the fixed term, the Lodger may end the agreement early by giving at least two months' written notice, provided that the notice does not expire before 28 February 2027.
8.2 After the fixed term, either party may end the agreement by giving one month's written notice, to expire on the last day of a rental period.
8.3 Notice may be given by email to fiona.marsh.lets@example.com or by letter.
8.4 The Householder may end the agreement with 7 days' notice if the Lodger is more than one month behind with rent or seriously breaches clause 4.

9. Signed
Fiona Marsh, 12 August 2026
Tomasz Wiśniewski, 12 August 2026
