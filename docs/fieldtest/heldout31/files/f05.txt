kyoto trip notes!! (nov 8-12)

getting there
- shinkansen from tokyo, nozomi ~2h15. we're NOT getting the JR pass, price went up a lot, just buying single tickets.
- load ICOCA / suica on phone for buses + subway. buses in kyoto get super packed, maybe use subway + walk.

stay: small ryokan near Higashiyama, 2 nights with kaiseki dinner, then cheap hotel near Kyoto Station for last 2 nights. remember to take shoes off, yukata for dinner is fine

day 1 (arrive afternoon)
- Kiyomizu-dera at sunset, then walk down Sannenzaka / Ninenzaka
- evening walk through Gion, Hanamikoji street. DO NOT take photos of geiko/maiko without asking, they've put up signs and fines for private alleys

day 2
- Fushimi Inari EARLY (before 7:30) - thousands of torii gates, full hike to the top ~2-3 hrs. Inari = fox shrine?
- Tofuku-ji for autumn leaves (momiji) if they've turned
- afternoon: Nishiki Market for snacks, then Pontocho for dinner

day 3
- Arashiyama bamboo grove early, Tenryu-ji garden, Togetsukyo bridge
- train back to center, Kinkaku-ji (golden pavilion) - closes 5pm
- maybe Ryoan-ji rock garden nearby

day 4
- day trip to Nara? deer park + Todai-ji big buddha. 45 min on Kintetsu
- or Philosopher's Path + Ginkaku-ji (silver pavilion, not actually silver lol)

misc
- temples charge 400-1000 yen entry, bring cash
- goshuin book for temple stamps
- tea ceremony in Uji? matcha everything
- so many temples and shrines here vs tokyo - why?? i thought tokyo was the capital. ask Kenji

budget ~ 18,000 yen/day not including ryokan
