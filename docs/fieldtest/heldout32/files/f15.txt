Not long now! Here is everything you need to sort before you fly on Wednesday 18 November (BA005, London Heathrow 13:05, arriving Tokyo Haneda 09:50 the next day).

1. Passports
Your passports must be valid for the whole time you are in Japan. Gareth, yours expires in February 2027, which is fine. As British citizens you do not need a visa for a tourist stay of up to 90 days.

2. Visit Japan Web
Please register on Visit Japan Web before you travel and fill in the immigration and customs details for each of you. You will get QR codes to show at Haneda, which makes arrival much quicker. Do this at least 6 hours before your flight lands; I'd suggest the weekend before you go.

3. Japan Rail Pass
I have ordered two 14-day Green Car passes. You will receive exchange orders by courier around 26 October. Someone must be home to sign. Each exchange order must be exchanged for the actual pass in Japan within 3 months of issue. Exchange at the JR office at Haneda, and choose your start date carefully - I'd suggest starting on 20 November, the day you leave Tokyo for Kanazawa, as your first day in Tokyo can be done on the metro.

4. Medication
Lucy, you mentioned you take Elvanse. Lisdexamfetamine is a stimulant and is prohibited in Japan, even with a prescription. Please speak to your GP or specialist about an alternative for the trip as soon as possible. Also, some UK cold and flu medicines containing pseudoephedrine are banned, so don't pack Sudafed or similar. For any other prescription medicine, if you are bringing more than a one-month supply you will need an import certificate (Yunyu Kakunin-sho) applied for in advance.

5. Travel insurance
Your policy with Lumen Cover is confirmed, but it does not yet include winter sports. If you still want to do the snowshoe day in Hakuba, please add the winter sports extension (GBP 24 each) before departure.

6. Money
Japan is still quite cash-based outside the big cities. I'd take around JPY 50,000 each in cash and use 7-Eleven ATMs for top-ups. Load a Suica card on your phone for trains and convenience stores.

7. Balance
The remaining balance of GBP 2,340 is due by 20 October. You can pay by bank transfer (details on your invoice) or by card over the phone.

8. Luggage
I've booked luggage forwarding (takkyubin) for your big cases from the Tokyo hotel straight to your Kyoto hotel, so you can travel light to Kanazawa. Leave the cases at the Tokyo hotel reception by 10:00 on 20 November; they will be waiting for you in Kyoto when you arrive on 23 November. Pack an overnight bag for the three nights in Kanazawa.
