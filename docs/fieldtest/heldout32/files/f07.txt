ASSURED SHORTHOLD TENANCY AGREEMENT (extract - clauses 1 to 9)

Property: Flat 3, 14 Harcourt Road, Leeds LS6 2QF
Landlord: Mr Graham Pettigrew, c/o Northline Lettings Ltd, 88 Otley Road, Leeds LS6 4BA ("the Agent")
Tenant: Ms Chiara Benedetti

1. TERM
The tenancy is for a fixed term of 12 months starting on 1 February 2026 and ending on 31 January 2027.

2. RENT
The rent is GBP 950 per calendar month, payable in advance on the 1st day of each month by standing order to the Agent. Each rental period runs from the 1st day to the last day of a calendar month.

3. DEPOSIT
A deposit of GBP 1,096 has been paid and is protected with the Deposit Protection Service (DPS), certificate no. DPS-7731-0492. It will be returned within 10 days of agreement on deductions after the end of the tenancy.

4. TENANT BREAK CLAUSE
4.1 The Tenant may end this tenancy early, provided the tenancy has run for at least 6 months, by giving the Agent not less than two months' written notice.
4.2 The notice must expire on the last day of a rental period. For example, to end the tenancy on 30 June, notice must be received by the Agent on or before 30 April.
4.3 Rent remains payable up to and including the date the notice expires.
4.4 The break clause is conditional on the Tenant having no rent arrears on the date the notice expires.

5. SERVICE OF NOTICES
5.1 Notices must be in writing and sent either by post to the Agent's address above or by email to tenancies@northline-lettings.co.uk.
5.2 A notice sent by email is treated as received on the day it is sent if it is sent before 5pm on a working day (Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays); otherwise it is treated as received on the next working day.
5.3 A notice sent by first class post is treated as received on the second working day after posting.

6. UTILITIES AND COUNCIL TAX
The Tenant is responsible for gas, electricity, water, broadband, TV licence and council tax for the duration of the tenancy.

7. PETS
No pets without the Landlord's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld. A cat was approved on 12 February 2026.

8. REPAIRS AND ACCESS
The Tenant must report repairs to the Agent promptly. The Landlord or Agent may enter the Property on giving at least 24 hours' written notice, except in an emergency.

9. END OF TENANCY
The Tenant must return all keys (2 front door, 1 communal, 1 bike store) to the Agent by 12 noon on the last day of the tenancy. Professional cleaning is not required, but the Property must be returned in the same condition as at the start, allowing for fair wear and tear, as recorded in the inventory dated 30 January 2026.
