Year 8 Science Trip - Natural History Museum, London
Thursday 12 November 2026

As part of our "Evolution and Inheritance" topic, all Year 8 students are invited on a day trip to the Natural History Museum. Students will visit the Human Evolution and Dinosaurs galleries and take part in a 45-minute workshop on fossils led by museum staff.

Timings
Students should arrive at school by 7:30am; the coaches leave from the bus bay at 7:45am prompt. We expect to return at approximately 5:30pm. If there are delays, we will post updates on the school app. Please collect your child from the bus bay; students will not be allowed to walk home alone unless we have your written permission on the consent form.

Cost
The trip costs GBP 18 per student, covering coach travel and the workshop. Please pay via ParentPay by Friday 30 October. If the cost is a difficulty for your family, please contact Mrs Delaney in the finance office in confidence - nobody will miss out for financial reasons.

Consent
Please complete the online consent form (link on the school app) by Friday 23 October. Include any medical needs, allergies and medication. Students who carry inhalers or adrenaline auto-injectors (EpiPens) MUST bring them on the day; they will not be allowed on the coach without them.

What to bring
- A packed lunch and a drink in a disposable bag or container. There will be no opportunity to buy lunch at the museum. Students entitled to free school meals can order a free packed lunch from the school kitchen - tick the box on the consent form by 23 October.
- No glass bottles, fizzy drinks or energy drinks.
- A waterproof coat.
- Up to GBP 10 spending money for the gift shop (optional).
- A pen and clipboard - worksheets will be handed out on the coach.

Uniform
Students must wear full school uniform, including blazers. PE kit or trainers are not allowed.

Mobile phones
Phones may be brought but must be switched off on the coach and in the galleries. They may be used to take photos during the lunch break only. The school cannot accept responsibility for loss or damage.

Behaviour
This is a curriculum trip and our usual behaviour policy applies. Students who have received a level 3 behaviour sanction this half term may not be able to attend; Heads of Year will contact the families concerned by 26 October.

If you have any questions, please contact the trip leader, Mr Farouk (Head of Biology), at science@hollinswood.sch.uk.
