# greenhouse-node

Battery-powered temperature / humidity / soil-moisture node for the allotment greenhouse. Sends readings over WiFi to the Home Assistant box in the shed via MQTT.

## Hardware

- Board: FireBeetle ESP32-E (chosen for its low deep-sleep current; the regulator on the old DevKit drew ~8 mA even in sleep)
- Sensors: SHT31 (temp + humidity, I2C, addr 0x44) and a capacitive soil moisture probe v2.0 on GPIO 34 (ADC1)
- Power: 2x AA lithium (Energizer Ultimate, 3000 mAh each) in series -> 3.0-3.6 V straight into the 3V3 pin, no regulator
- Soil probe is powered from GPIO 25 so it is only on while measuring (it draws 5 mA continuously otherwise)

## How it works

1. Wake from deep sleep (RTC timer).
2. Power the soil probe, wait 300 ms, read ADC (average of 10 samples), read SHT31.
3. Connect to WiFi (static IP 192.168.4.37 to skip DHCP - saves ~1 s), publish JSON to `greenhouse/node1/state`, disconnect.
4. Deep sleep until the next interval.

The interval is set by `SLEEP_MINUTES` in `config.h` (default 15).

## Power budget (measured with the Nordic PPK2, 12 Sept 2026)

| State | Current | Time |
|-------|---------|------|
| Awake (sensors + WiFi + MQTT publish) | 120 mA average | 4 s per wake |
| Deep sleep | 25 uA (0.025 mA) | rest of the time |

Usable capacity: assume 80% of 3000 mAh = 2400 mAh (cold greenhouse in winter, and the voltage cut-off at about 2.9 V).
Note: two AA cells in series give 3000 mAh at ~3 V, not 6000 mAh - capacity only adds in parallel.

Worked example at the default 15-minute interval:
- 96 wakes per day x 4 s = 384 s = 0.107 h awake
- Awake: 0.107 h x 120 mA = 12.8 mAh/day
- Sleep: about 24 h x 0.025 mA = 0.6 mAh/day
- Total: about 13.4 mAh/day -> 2400 / 13.4 = about 179 days (roughly 6 months)

If WiFi takes longer to connect (weak signal at the far end of the greenhouse), awake time can go up to 7-8 s and battery life drops accordingly. Keep the node within ~15 m of the shed access point.

## Calibration

Soil probe raw ADC values (12-bit): dry air ~3150, glass of water ~1350. `config.h` maps these to 0-100%. Recalibrate if you change the probe or cable length.

## Known issues

- SHT31 reads ~0.8 C high if the node is in direct sun; the 3D-printed radiation shield (stl/shield_v3.stl) fixes this.
- After a brown-out the RTC memory is lost and the boot counter resets - harmless.
- MQTT broker must allow retained messages, otherwise Home Assistant shows "unknown" until the next wake.
