Offer of employment - Junior Data Analyst

Following your interview on 6 October, I am delighted to offer you the position of Junior Data Analyst in our Planning & Insights team, reporting to Meera Kaul, Analytics Lead. This offer is subject to two satisfactory references and proof of your right to work in the UK.

Start date: Monday 2 November 2026.

Salary: GBP 31,500 per year, paid monthly in arrears on the last working day of each month. Salaries are reviewed each April; you will be eligible for your first review in April 2027.

Hours and location: 37.5 hours per week, Monday to Friday, core hours 10:00 to 16:00. The role is hybrid: you are expected in the Bristol office on Tuesdays and Thursdays and may work from home on the other days. A laptop and monitor will be provided.

Probationary period: your first 6 months of employment will be a probationary period. We may extend it by up to a further 3 months if we need more time to assess your performance. Holiday: 25 days per year plus UK bank holidays. The holiday year runs January to December, so for 2026 you will receive a pro-rata entitlement of 4 days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year.

Pension: you will be enrolled in the company pension scheme after 3 months. You contribute 5% of qualifying earnings and the company contributes 4%. You may opt out within one month of enrolment.

Other benefits: life assurance at 2x salary, a cycle-to-work scheme, and a training budget of GBP 750 per year after probation.

Sick pay: statutory sick pay during probation; after probation, full pay for up to 4 weeks in any rolling 12-month period.

To accept, please sign and return the enclosed copy of this letter and the new starter form by Friday 23 October 2026. Your full contract of employment will be issued on your first day.

We are very much looking forward to working with you.
