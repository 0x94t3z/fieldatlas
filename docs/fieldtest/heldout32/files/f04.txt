YOUR DOSE
METHOTREXATE 15 mg (six x 2.5 mg tablets) ONCE A WEEK, on MONDAYS.
FOLIC ACID 5 mg ONCE A WEEK, on THURSDAYS. Do not take folic acid on the same day as your methotrexate.

Methotrexate is a WEEKLY medicine. Taking it every day by mistake can cause serious harm, including severe damage to the blood cells, liver and gut, and can be fatal. Only ever take it on your chosen day.

IF YOU MISS A DOSE
If you remember within 2 days (by Wednesday), take it as soon as you remember and carry on with your usual Monday dose the following week. If you remember later than that, miss out that week's dose completely and take your next dose on the usual Monday. Never take a double dose to make up for a missed one. If you are unsure, call the rheumatology advice line on 01632 960 441 (Mon-Fri 9am-4pm; leave a message and we will call back within one working day).

BLOOD TESTS
You need a full blood count, kidney and liver tests every 2 weeks until you have been on a stable dose for 6 weeks, then monthly for 3 months, then every 3 months. Bring your monitoring booklet to every test. Your next review is on 2 November 2026; if your blood results are satisfactory your dose may be increased to 20 mg once a week.

ALCOHOL
Keep to no more than 14 units a week, and ideally much less, because methotrexate and alcohol can both affect the liver.

INFECTIONS AND SIDE EFFECTS
Stop methotrexate and contact us or your GP the same day if you develop a fever, sore throat, mouth ulcers, unexplained bruising or bleeding, breathlessness or a new dry cough. Do not restart until you have been told it is safe. Mild nausea on the day after your dose is common; taking the tablets in the evening may help.

OTHER MEDICINES
Always tell any doctor, dentist or pharmacist that you take methotrexate. Do NOT take trimethoprim or co-trimoxazole (Septrin), as these interact dangerously with methotrexate.

VACCINES
Avoid live vaccines (for example yellow fever and oral typhoid). The yearly flu vaccine, COVID-19 boosters and the Shingrix shingles vaccine are not live and are recommended.

PREGNANCY
Methotrexate can harm an unborn baby. Use reliable contraception and talk to the team at least 3 months before trying for a baby.
