Re: Mr Leonard FAIRBROTHER, DOB 19/11/1944, 14 Castle View

Mr Fairbrother attended the memory clinic on 29 September 2026 with his daughter Clare. He describes 18 months of gradually worsening word-finding difficulty and misplacing items, and he has missed two hospital appointments this year. He remains independent in personal care and cooking, still drives locally, and since the spring has managed his finances with Clare's help.

Past medical history: hypertension, type 2 diabetes (diet controlled), bilateral hearing loss (wears hearing aids), ex-smoker (stopped 1998).
Medication: ramipril 5 mg once daily, aspirin 75 mg once daily.

Assessment
MoCA 23/30 (16 years of education, so no point added). He lost points on delayed recall (2/5), clock drawing (1/3), serial sevens (2/3) and verbal fluency (0/1). Orientation was full. ACE-III not completed because of time. GDS-15 score 3, not suggestive of depression.
Bloods (September): B12, folate, thyroid function and calcium normal. HbA1c 47 mmol/mol.
CT head (22 September 2026): moderate small vessel ischaemic change and mild generalised atrophy. No mass or haemorrhage.
Clinic blood pressure: 152/86.

Impression
Mild cognitive impairment (MCI), probably with a vascular contribution. He does not currently meet criteria for dementia because his day-to-day function is largely preserved.

Plan
1. No anti-dementia medication is indicated at present.
2. Optimise vascular risk factors: BP target below 140/90. Please consider increasing ramipril to 10 mg. A statin was discussed; he would like time to think about it.
3. Hearing aids checked and new batteries fitted; re-referral to audiology advised, as poor hearing can lower test scores and add to his difficulties.
4. Driving was discussed. He has agreed to stop driving at night, and Clare will let us know of any concerns.
5. He has been offered a place on our cognitive stimulation group: 6 weekly sessions on Wednesdays at 10:00, starting 4 November 2026.
6. Review in the memory clinic in 12 months, or sooner if there are concerns.
