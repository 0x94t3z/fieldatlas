Important changes to your account

Dear Ms Raman,

We're writing to tell you about some changes to your Calder Plus Current Account. Please read this letter and keep it with your account terms.

1. Arranged overdraft interest rate - from 1 December 2026
The interest rate on your arranged overdraft will change from 35.9% EAR (variable) to 39.9% EAR (variable).
The first GBP 50 of your arranged overdraft will continue to be interest-free. This interest-free buffer is not changing and applies to all our current accounts.
Representative example (from 1 December 2026): if you use an arranged overdraft of GBP 1,200, the interest rate is 39.9% EAR (variable). Your current arranged overdraft limit is GBP 1,000, and that limit is not changing.

2. Monthly account fee - from 1 January 2027
The monthly fee for the Calder Plus Current Account will increase from GBP 3.00 to GBP 4.00. The account will continue to include worldwide travel insurance and breakdown cover. The fee will not be charged in any month in which you pay in at least GBP 2,000.

3. Unarranged overdrafts - no change
We do not offer unarranged overdrafts. If you try to make a payment and there isn't enough money in your account or available arranged overdraft, we will usually refuse the payment. We do not charge a fee for refused payments.

4. Interest on credit balances - no change
Credit interest remains at 1.00% AER (variable) on balances up to GBP 1,500.

What you need to do
You don't need to do anything. If you're happy with the changes, they will take effect automatically on the dates above.

If you don't want to accept the changes, you can switch to another Calder account or close your account at any time before the changes take effect, without charge. You can also use the Current Account Switch Service to move to another provider. If you have an overdraft balance, you will need to repay it before closing your account.

If you have questions, call us on 0345 300 1277 (Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm) or visit any Calder branch.
