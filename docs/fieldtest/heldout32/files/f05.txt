RIVERSIDE PHYSIOTHERAPY - HOME EXERCISE PROGRAMME

Patient: Derek Mallon        Physiotherapist: Sian Roberts (MCSP)
Condition: osteoarthritis, left knee

Programme start date: Tuesday 29 September 2026.
Weeks are counted from the start date (week 1 = 29 Sep - 5 Oct, week 2 starts 6 Oct, and so on).

PAIN MONITORING
Score your knee pain from 0 (none) to 10 (worst imaginable) while you exercise.
- 0-3: fine, carry on.
- 4-5: acceptable only if it settles back to your normal level within 24 hours.
- Above 5: too much. Drop back to the previous stage for a week.

STAGE 1 (weeks 1-2) - every day
- Static quads: tighten the thigh, hold 5 seconds, 10 reps, 3 sets
- Straight leg raise: 10 reps, 3 sets
- Heel slides lying on the bed: 15 reps
- Sit-to-stand from a high chair, hands on knees if needed: 8 reps, 2 sets

STAGE 2 (weeks 3-5) - 5 days a week
Keep the stage 1 exercises and ADD:
- Step-ups onto the bottom stair (about 15 cm), holding the banister: 10 reps, 2 sets, leading with each leg.
  Only start the step-ups if your pain during the stage 1 exercises is 3/10 or lower AND you have had no knee swelling lasting more than 24 hours after exercising.
- Mini squats holding the kitchen worktop: 10 reps, 2 sets
- Exercise bike, 10 minutes, low resistance (if you have access to one)

STAGE 3 (week 6 onwards) - 3 to 4 days a week
- Increase step height to 20 cm
- Add the yellow resistance band to sit-to-stands and leg raises
- Walking target: 30 minutes continuous, flat ground

OTHER ADVICE
- You may take paracetamol 30 minutes before exercising, as agreed with your GP.
- An ice pack (wrapped in a tea towel) for 10-15 minutes afterwards can help if the knee is sore.
- Wear supportive trainers for step-ups and walking.

Stop and contact us on 01904 555 218 if the knee locks, gives way, or becomes hot, red and swollen.
