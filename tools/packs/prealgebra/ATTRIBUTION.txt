Mathematics: Prealgebra collection for Field Atlas (`openstax-maths-stats`).

This collection is built from the book:

Prealgebra 2e
Senior Contributing Authors: Lynn Marecek and MaryAnne Anthony-Smith
Contributing Author: Andrea Honeycutt Mathis
OpenStax, Rice University
https://openstax.org/details/books/prealgebra-2e

The text is OpenStax's own, kept whole, one passage per numbered book
section, each followed by its glossary. Changes made: formulas are carried
as LaTeX converted from the book's MathML, tables are written one row to a
line, the circled letters that label the parts of a question are written
"(a)", "(b)", and images are left out (their captions and descriptions are
kept).
The preface, the chapter introductions and the appendices are not included.
No OpenStax logo is used, and OpenStax does not endorse Field Atlas.
