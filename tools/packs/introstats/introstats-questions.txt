Field Atlas test questions for the OpenStax Introductory Statistics 2e collection
Five questions, written before the answers run. Expected answers were computed
independently of the collection; the run retrieves the teaching section for
each question from the built pack and works the answer from it.

1. In a survey of 200 students, 45 said they take the bus to campus. What is the relative frequency of students who take the bus?
2. An inspector samples n = 20 items. Each item is defective with probability p = 0.3, independently. If X is the number of defective items, X ~ B(20, 0.3). What are the mean and standard deviation of X?
3. Heights are normally distributed with mean 170 cm and standard deviation 10 cm. By the Empirical Rule, about what percent of heights lie between 150 cm and 190 cm?
4. A sample of n = 36 has mean x-bar = 82. The population standard deviation is known, sigma = 12. What is the 95% confidence interval for the population mean?
5. A survey gives the 2x2 table: group A answers 30 yes / 20 no; group B answers 10 yes / 40 no (row totals 50 and 50, column totals 40 and 60, grand total 100). For a chi-square test of independence, what are the expected frequencies, the test statistic, and the degrees of freedom?
