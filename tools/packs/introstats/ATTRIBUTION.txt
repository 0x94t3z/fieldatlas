Mathematics: Introductory Statistics collection for Field Atlas
(`openstax-maths-introstats`).

This collection is built from the book:

Introductory Statistics 2e
Senior Contributing Authors: Barbara Illowsky and Susan Dean (De Anza College)
Contributing Authors: Daniel Birmajer, Bryan Blount, Sheri Boyd, Matthew
Einsohn, Nancy Foreman, James Helmreich, Lynette Kenyon, Sheldon Lee and
Jeff Taub
OpenStax, Rice University
https://openstax.org/details/books/introductory-statistics-2e

The text is OpenStax's own, kept whole, one passage per numbered book
section, each followed by its glossary. Changes made: formulas are carried
as LaTeX converted from the book's MathML, tables are written one row to a
line, the circled letters that label the parts of a question are written
"(a)", "(b)", and images are left out (their captions and descriptions are
kept). The preface, the chapter introductions and six appendices are not
included. The two appendices that hold the book's formula tables are kept:
Appendix F (Mathematical Phrases, Symbols, and Formulas) and Appendix H
(Tables). No OpenStax logo is used, and OpenStax does not endorse Field Atlas.
