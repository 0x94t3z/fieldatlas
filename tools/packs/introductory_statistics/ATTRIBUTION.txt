Mathematics: Introductory statistics collection for Field Atlas (`openstax-introductory-statistics`).

This collection is built from the book:

Introductory Statistics 2e
Senior Contributing Authors: Barbara Illowsky and Susan Dean, De Anza College
OpenStax, Rice University
https://openstax.org/details/books/introductory-statistics-2e

The text is OpenStax's own, kept whole, one passage per module: each
chapter's introduction and every numbered section (the labs included),
with its worked examples, exercises, chapter review, formula review,
practice, homework and the book's solutions, each followed by its
glossary; and the appendices "Mathematical Phrases, Symbols, and
Formulas" and "Tables". Changes made: formulas are carried as LaTeX
converted from the book's MathML, tables are written one row to a line,
images are left out (their captions and descriptions are kept), a
summation sign with an empty superscript is written as the plain sign,
and two characters from the Symbol font's private-use area are written as
the characters they stand for (alpha and minus).
The preface and the other appendices (review exercises, practice tests,
data sets, projects, solution sheets, calculator notes) are not included.
No OpenStax logo is used, and OpenStax does not endorse Field Atlas.
