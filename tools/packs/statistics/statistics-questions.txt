Field Atlas test questions for the OpenStax Introductory Statistics 2e collection
(poidh bounty, Base 1554). Thirty questions, written before the pack build,
each answerable from the book's own text. Answers are recorded separately
with the section cited for each.

1. What is the difference between a population and a sample?
2. What level of measurement is hair color: nominal, ordinal, interval, or ratio?
3. In a designed experiment, what is the difference between the explanatory variable and the response variable?
4. What is the median of the data set 2, 4, 6, 8, 10?
5. What five values does a box plot display?
6. In a right-skewed distribution, is the mean greater than, less than, or equal to the median?
7. If P(A) = 0.3 and P(B) = 0.4 and A and B are independent, what is P(A AND B)?
8. What is the addition rule for the probability of A OR B?
9. What is a contingency table used for?
10. What must the sum of all probabilities in a discrete probability distribution function equal?
11. For a binomial distribution with n = 10 trials and p = 0.5, what is the expected value (mean)?
12. For a Poisson distribution, what is the mean in terms of its parameter?
13. What is the probability density function of a uniform distribution on the interval [a, b]?
14. For an exponential distribution with decay parameter m, what is the mean?
15. What is the z-score formula?
16. Approximately what area under the standard normal curve lies between z = -1 and z = 1?
17. What z-score corresponds to the 90th percentile of the standard normal distribution?
18. What does the Central Limit Theorem say about the sampling distribution of sample means as the sample size grows?
19. What is the formula for the standard error of the mean?
20. What is the formula for the error bound for a population mean (EBM) when the population standard deviation is known?
21. What is the z-score used for a 95% confidence interval?
22. As the sample size increases, does a confidence interval get wider or narrower?
23. What is a Type I error in hypothesis testing?
24. What is the null hypothesis?
25. What significance level is most commonly used, and what does a p-value smaller than it mean?
26. Which test compares the means of two independent populations when the population standard deviations are unknown?
27. What is a matched-pairs (paired samples) test used for?
28. What is the chi-square goodness-of-fit test used for?
29. For a chi-square test of independence with r rows and c columns, what are the degrees of freedom?
30. What is the least-squares regression line, and what does the correlation coefficient r measure?
